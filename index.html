Our approach

No two treatments
need to look the same.

No two sore backs are alike, and no two shoulders respond in quite the same way. That's why we listen first and take a proper look before we start treating.

Sometimes that means manual therapy, sometimes targeted exercise – often a bit of both. We'll tell you honestly what we're seeing, and what makes sense as the next step.

Between sessions

What you do at home matters just as much.

A treatment session can give things a real push in the right direction. But lasting change usually happens between appointments.

That's why we'll show you exercises and movements you can keep doing at home – say, gentle mobility work for back pain or strengthening exercises after an injury. We'll talk through what makes sense for you during your appointment, and adjust it as things change.

Lymphatic drainage

Gentle treatment,
used where it counts.

If you're dealing with swelling or a lymphatic condition, manual lymphatic drainage can help move fluid out of the tissue and ease your symptoms.

It's often used after surgery, for lipoedema, or for swelling caused by poor lymphatic flow. The treatment is very gentle – you simply lie back and relax while we work on the affected area.

Before your first session, we'll walk you through what to expect and tailor the therapy to your situation.
